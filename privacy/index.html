Privacy Policy

Nanofy — Photo & PDF Compressor · Last updated 18 August 2026

Nanofy is a photo and PDF compression app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. It applies to both platform versions.

The short version

Your photos and PDFs are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not have an account system, we do not collect your files, and we never see their contents or their names.

Nanofy is free. This version shows no advertisements and contains no analytics, so there is nothing about you for us to collect.

Your files

  • Compression, conversion, resizing and all PDF operations run locally on your device.
  • Your files are never transmitted to Nanofy or to any server.
  • File names and file contents are never sent to analytics or advertising systems.
  • Working copies are kept in the app's private cache while an operation is in progress and are removed automatically within 24 hours, or when you clear the app's cache.
  • Files you explicitly save are written to your photo library (images) or your device storage (PDFs). They stay under your control.

Access we request

WhatWhy
Photo selection Nanofy uses the system photo picker. It only receives the specific photos you choose, and it does not request access to your whole photo library.
Saving photos On iOS, add-only permission to your photo library so a compressed copy can be saved. Nanofy cannot read your library with this permission. On Android 9 and older, a storage permission is required to write to your Pictures folder; on Android 10 and newer no permission is used.
PDF selection The system document picker. Nanofy only receives the files you pick.
Internet Not used by this version for your files or your data. Your files are never sent anywhere.

Advertising and analytics

This version of Nanofy shows no advertisements, and we run no analytics of our own. We do not build a profile of you and we do not track you across apps or websites.

A future version may introduce advertising to keep the app free. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the store listing's data disclosure will be updated to match, and — in Europe, the UK and Switzerland — you will be asked for consent inside the app first.

Children

Nanofy is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because Nanofy has no account and stores nothing about you on any server, there is no personal data held by us to delete. Removing the app removes its cache and any settings it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
